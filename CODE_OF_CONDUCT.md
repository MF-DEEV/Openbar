# Code of conduct

Openbar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work, and make room for people who are new.

## Scope

It applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer, [@MF-DEEV](https://github.com/MF-DEEV), privately. Use the [private advisory form](https://github.com/MF-DEEV/Openbar/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct:" at the start of the title. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may warn or ban the people who wrote them, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
